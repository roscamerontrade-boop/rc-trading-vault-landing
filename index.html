Straight answers

The questions everyone asks before joining — answered the same way we answer them in the server.

Is this authorised, or pirated content?

Authorised. RC Trading Vault resells these courses with permission confirmed from the original sellers, and free sample lessons are shared with the creators' permission too — which is why courses and creators are always named and credited, never hidden.

Why is it so much cheaper than the original price?

Our pricing rule is public: Vault price ≈ 15% of the original, rounded to a fair number, and capped at $90 maximum. FVGC Premium is $749 from Capital Complex — it's $90 here. Same course, Vault price.

What do I get for free, exactly?

A full sample FVG lesson from FVGC Premium (on YouTube and in #free-videos), the 4 beginner guides in #free-resources, daily free market posts, and access to the public channels — including the full priced catalogue, so you can compare before spending anything.

How do I actually buy, and how do I get access?

Join the Discord, check #course-catalogue, then open a ticket in #open-ticket. The team confirms what you're buying and the exact price, takes payment, and unlocks the private channels for your courses. Paid access is granted after verified payment — never on a claim alone.

Is membership access lifetime?

It depends what you buy, and we'll always say which: one-time and "lifetime" courses are a single payment; memberships (Starter, Plus, monthly courses) run while your subscription is active. Nothing is dressed up as lifetime when it isn't.

Will this make me profitable?

Nobody honest can promise that, and we don't. This is trading education — concepts, chart reading, risk and process. What you do with it, and your results, are yours. Anyone guaranteeing returns is lying to you.

What if something goes wrong — access, payment, refunds?

Open a ticket. Support runs on six categories — course purchases, membership enquiries, payment assistance, access problems, general support, and refund & cancellation enquiries — so your issue lands with the right process from the start.
